Help & Support

Answers to common questions, how to manage your subscription, and how to reach us. In the app, More › How to use Buddy has a short how-to for every page, each with a Show me button.

Contact

Something not working, or an idea? Email akhoale@gmail.com, or use Settings › About › Send feedback in the app. Please include your device and its iOS, iPadOS, macOS or watchOS version, and the app version from Settings › About.

Manage or cancel a subscription

On iPhone or iPad

  • In the app: Settings › Subscription › Manage subscription, or
  • In the Settings app: tap your name › Subscriptions › Buddy Portfolio.

On Mac

  • Open the App Store, click your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then Account Settings › Subscriptions › Manage.

Good to know

  • Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid the next charge. You keep your plan until the period ends.
  • On a free week (yearly plans), cancel at least 24 hours before it ends and you won't be charged.
  • Restore purchases: Settings › Subscription › See plans › Restore purchases. One subscription works on iPhone, iPad and Mac with the same Apple Account.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • If a plan ends, everything you've added stays. Above the Basic limit you can view and remove stocks but not add more.

Common questions

Does Buddy give investment advice?

No. It never tells you what to do with your money. It describes how a stock or your portfolio looks today. Grades and outlooks are checks, not recommendations. For information only; not investment, tax or legal advice.

Do I need an account?

No. Sign in with Apple is optional.

How do I add my stocks?

Tap + at the top of Home or Holdings, type a ticker or a company name, then the number of shares. The price you paid is optional; add it to see your total gain. To follow a stock you don't own, add it to your Watchlist. Coins go in More › Crypto › Add a coin. On Mac: ⌘N adds a stock and ⇧⌘N adds one to your watchlist.

Can I import my holdings from my broker?

With Plus or Pro: tap the import button at the top of Holdings (on Mac, ⇧⌘I). Pick an Excel, CSV or PDF file, or a screenshot from your broker. It's read on your device and never uploaded. Check the list, then tap Add.

What do the grades mean?

Every stock gets two grades, A to F: short term (about a month) and long term (about a year). A Positive, Neutral or Negative outlook sums them up. Green Good, amber OK and red Bad tags show which numbers help and which hurt, and Valuation reads below, near or above fair value. Tap Explain it simply on any card whose numbers aren't obvious; Learn has every word.

Why can't I open a second stock in Stock Analysis?

Basic includes one stock a day, and reopening that stock is free. Plus and Pro have no limit.

Where do my stocks live?

On your device, and in your own private iCloud if sync is on. The developer can't see them. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I sync between iPhone, iPad and Mac?

Sign in to the same Apple Account on each device and turn on Settings › iCloud sync › Sync with my other devices on each one. Changes usually arrive within a minute. The Apple Watch gets its data from your iPhone.

Can I hide my dollar amounts quickly?

Yes. Tap your portfolio value on Home, use Settings › Privacy › Hide dollar amounts, or say "Hide my amounts in Buddy Analyst." Amounts and share counts become $•••; percentages stay. The Lock Screen has its own settings: Settings › Widgets & controls › Portfolio value on Lock Screen widgets, and Settings › Live Activities › Dollar gain/loss.

Does the AI send my portfolio anywhere?

No. Apple Intelligence runs on your device. It needs iPhone 15 Pro or later, or an iPad or Mac with an M-series or A17 Pro chip, with Apple Intelligence turned on. Without it, Buddy writes its explanations from its own numbers, so every card still works.

The Live Activity isn't showing
  1. Check iPhone Settings › Buddy Analyst › Live Activities is on.
  2. In Buddy, turn Settings › Live Activities › Market hours on the Lock Screen off and on again. iOS ends Live Activities after about 8 hours.
  3. Low Power Mode, or Background App Refresh being off, can slow background updates. The activity always shows when it last updated.
  4. You can follow up to 3 stocks or coins at once; stop following one before adding another.
Siri doesn't answer
  1. Include the app's name: "… in Buddy Analyst." (Plain "Buddy" is a different app, Buddy Reader.)
  2. Open Buddy once after installing or updating.
  3. Check iPhone Settings › Buddy Analyst › Siri › Use with Ask Siri.
Widgets aren't updating

Widgets update after Buddy refreshes prices, and iOS decides how often. Opening Buddy, or the Refresh prices control, updates them right away. If a widget stays blank, remove it and add it again. If the value is missing on the Lock Screen, check Settings › Widgets & controls and privacy mode.

Notifications don't arrive
  1. In Buddy, go to Settings › Notifications and tap Allow notifications (or Open Settings if you said no before).
  2. Check quiet hours in Settings › Smart alerts.
  3. Add Buddy Analyst to your Focus's allowed apps (iPhone Settings › Focus).
  4. Use Send test smart alerts in Settings › Smart alerts to check.
Prices look old, or I'm offline

Buddy opens with your last prices and keeps working offline. Tap the little clock to see when prices came in, or pull down to refresh. Quotes can be delayed by up to 15 minutes. On market holidays the market is closed; the Calendar shows them.

How do I install the Apple Watch app?

On your iPhone, open the Watch app, find Buddy Analyst under Available Apps and tap Install. Turn the Digital Crown to move between pages. For complications, touch and hold the watch face, tap Edit and pick Buddy Analyst.

How do I delete my account?

Signing in is optional. If you signed in with Apple: Settings › Account & security › Delete account. To fully disconnect, also go to iPhone Settings › your name › Sign in with Apple › Buddy Analyst. Your stocks stay on your devices and in your iCloud.

How do I see what changed in an update?

Settings › About › What's new. It also opens once after each update.
