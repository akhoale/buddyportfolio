Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

Buddy Portfolio Analyst ("the app") needs no account and has no ads, no tracking and no third-party analytics. The developer does not collect personal information about you.

In short

  • Your holdings, watchlist, notes, goals and alerts are stored on your devices and, if you turn on iCloud sync, in your own private iCloud. The developer can't see them.
  • To show prices and grades, the app asks its data service for ticker symbols only (like AAPL).
  • AI features run on your device with Apple Intelligence where available.

What stays on your device

Your holdings (tickers, number of shares, the price you paid and the date), watchlist, notes, goals, targets, alerts, trophies and settings are saved in the app on your iPhone, iPad or Mac. Deleting the app deletes them from that device. Your Apple Watch, widgets and Live Activities get a short summary (for example your portfolio's value and today's move) from the app on the same device or your paired iPhone; it isn't sent anywhere else.

iCloud sync (optional)

When iCloud sync is on, your holdings, watchlist and alerts are kept in your private iCloud database (Apple's CloudKit) so they match on all your devices. Only you can access that database; the developer can't. Apple's privacy policy applies to iCloud. You can turn sync off at any time.

Market data

To show prices, grades, lists, news headlines and Market Health, the app requests data for the stocks you look at. These requests contain ticker symbols only, never your number of shares, what you paid, your portfolio's value or who you are. Like any internet request, they come from your device's IP address. The data service uses them to answer the request and does not keep a record of them. Prices and company data come from a licensed market-data provider, which receives nothing about you. Calculations that involve your own holdings (gains, weights, goals, how much you overlap with an investor's public filings) are done on your device.

Live updates (off by default). If you turn on Settings › Live Activities › Live updates from the Buddy server, the app sends the server your tickers and share counts (no names, no amounts paid, no identity) so it can update the Lock Screen while the app is closed, and only while that switch is on.

Importing holdings from a file

When you import holdings, the export, PDF statement or screenshot you pick is read on your device only. It is never uploaded. You check every row before anything is added.

AI features

The AI Desk, bull and bear cases, headline summaries, the morning brief and the import helper use Apple Intelligence, which runs on your device. Where Apple Intelligence isn't available, the app writes its explanations from its own numbers, also on your device. A few Stock Analysis features (for example "Explain how it makes money" and the AI corner) can be answered by the Buddy server's AI instead; those requests send the ticker, the task and, for the thesis check, the thesis text you typed, nothing else. AI answers are labelled and can be wrong.

Sign in with Apple (optional)

You never need an account. If you sign in with Apple, the app keeps the user ID Apple gives it and, the first time only, the name and email Apple shares (which can be a private relay address). They are stored in your iCloud Keychain on your devices and are not sent to any server today. Settings › Account & security › Delete account removes them from all your devices; to fully disconnect, also go to iPhone Settings › your name › Sign in with Apple › Buddy Analyst.

Face ID lock (optional)

The lock uses Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode through Apple's system. The app only learns whether you unlocked. Biometric data never leaves the device and the app never has access to it.

Notifications

Alerts, reminders and the morning brief are local notifications created on your device. Nothing about them is sent anywhere.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. The developer doesn't receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information; the app only learns which plan is active.

Feedback and diagnostics

Send feedback opens an email you write and send yourself. Diagnostics are only shared if you choose to export and send them. Nothing is sent automatically.

No ads, no tracking, no analytics

The app shows no ads, doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites, doesn't use the advertising identifier, and contains no third-party analytics or advertising code. No personal information is sold or shared.

Links you open

Headlines open the publisher's article, and some cards link to company, Nasdaq or SEC pages, only when you tap them. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or trackers of its own. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date before the change takes effect in the app.
