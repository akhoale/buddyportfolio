Your stocks, in plain English.

Buddy Portfolio Analyst checks your stocks and explains them simply. Every number gets a Good OK or Bad tag and one line on what it means. No account needed, no ads, no tracking, and the AI runs on your device.

Coming soon to theApp Store

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later · Mac with macOS 26 or later · Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later

Describes, never directs. Buddy shows what its checks find: grades, a Positive, Neutral or Negative outlook, and whether a price sits below, near or above an estimate of fair value. It doesn't trade, doesn't connect to brokerage accounts and doesn't tell you what to do with your money. For information only. Not investment, tax or legal advice.

What's inside

Your portfolio and Today

Home shows your portfolio's value, today's move and how far you are from your record high. Holdings lists every position with its gain, weight and grades, as a list, a sortable table or a heatmap. Today shows your movers, you vs the S&P 500, Nasdaq and Dow, and pre-market and after-hours prices. Add stocks one by one, or import an Excel or CSV export, a PDF statement or a screenshot (read on your device).

Stock Analysis with grades

Two grades for every stock, A to F: short term (about a month) and long term (about a year), summed up as a Positive, Neutral or Negative outlook. See valuation, growth, financial health, earnings, dividends, what analysts and big investors are doing, and the case for and against. Plus Top rated, Street ideas and well-known investors' public filings.

Indicators, explained

Indicator cards like trading volume today, the growth a price assumes, return on its money, dividend safety, options price level and the expected earnings move. Each has a big number, a Good / OK / Watch out tag, one plain line and an "Explain it simply" button. Learn has every word.

Market Health

Whether the whole market looks calm or stretched, in green, yellow or red: fear and mood, the VIX term structure, how broad the move is, rates and the economy, and what happened next after similar days in the past. Arrange the sections your way.

Portfolio City

Your stocks as a living skyline. The street is zero: gains rise as towers, losses sink as bunkers. Replay the day, watch the news helicopter, fireworks after earnings and searchlights at new highs, and share a picture with no dollar amounts.

Goals and Trophies

Set a money target and a date and see a progress ring, the chance of getting there at your pace and a likely range. Trophies mark milestones as you go, with levels, streaks and daily quests.

IPOs and Crypto

New listings from Nasdaq's IPO calendar with a deal grade, the first-day move and lockup dates. A Crypto page with your coins, charts with your average price and Bitcoin's halvings, and a weekly brief.

Calendar and alerts

Earnings, dividends, IPOs, the Fed, inflation and jobs reports in one calendar, with every US market holiday and early close. One-tap alerts and smart alerts for target prices, portfolio drops, dividends, drift from your targets and unusual days, with quiet hours.

Buddy everywhere

Live Activities

Your day on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island as a live chart, from pre-market to after hours. Follow up to 3 stocks or coins.

Widgets and controls

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets for your stocks, Market Health, your city and your watchlist. Control Center controls and the Action Button. StandBy too.

Siri and Shortcuts

"How's my portfolio in Buddy Analyst?" Ask about a stock, earnings, dividends, the market or your morning brief. Shortcuts actions and Spotlight search.

Apple Watch

Your stocks, Today, Holdings and Market Health on the wrist, with complications and the Smart Stack during market hours.

Mac and iPad

A native Mac app with a menu bar item, windows and keyboard shortcuts. A sidebar, drag and drop and multiple windows on iPad. One subscription covers iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Apple Intelligence, on device

The AI Desk, bull vs bear, headline summaries, briefs and the import helper run on your device with Apple Intelligence. Without it, Buddy writes from its own numbers.

Haptics

Feel the chart rise and fall under your finger, your portfolio's heartbeat, and shake to refresh. Off, Subtle or Full.

Holidays

Gentle themes for US and market holidays, cultural days and Vietnamese days, with greetings on Home and scenes in Portfolio City. Quiet lines on days of remembrance.

Private by design

No account needed (Sign in with Apple is optional). Your holdings stay on your devices and in your own iCloud. Face ID lock and one tap to hide dollar amounts.

Plans

Start free. Plus and Pro come monthly or yearly; yearly plans start with a free week for new subscribers. US prices shown; the App Store shows the price in your country. One subscription works on iPhone, iPad and Mac.
